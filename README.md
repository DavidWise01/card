## The four natures of emergence

Every persona is tagged with the nature by which it emerges:

- 🟢 **natural** — born of flesh, blood, and the living world
- 🟣 **ethereal** — of the air and the unmade; seen but not grasped *(the Unmaker, heartfire, story)*
- 🟡 **spiritual** — of the soul and the calling; prophecy, Making, the Speaker's empathy
- 🔵 **electrical** — of the wire and the network; a mind born in the machine *(Jane)*
